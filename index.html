          How to play

          1. Grow. Plant up to 3 seeds (free). They grow only when Squeektoshi runs. From Bud onwards, every settled run re-rolls each bloom's rarity. Bigger plants roll better.
          2. Pick. Picking freezes exactly what you see and turns it into a fighter. Wait for a better roll if you dare, but every plant wilts after 2,000 to 4,000 work.
          3. Raid. A pest boss is always attacking. The hamster hits it for 1 per unit of work. Arm an army of flowers: it waits for his next run, however long he sleeps, and multiplies that round's damage. Each boss is weak to one species (×2).
          4. It fights back. While the hamster sleeps, the boss bites your growing plants and shortens their lives, unless you station guards. When your army fights, it swipes: fighters can be knocked out for 36 rounds. Below half HP it enrages and bites and swipes double.
          5. Team up. Rally: 3+ gardeners in one round deal ×1.5. Combo: back-to-back rounds where he runs add +10% each, up to +50%. Crit: Poppies give a chance of ×2.
          6. Last hit wins. Armies in the killing round that brought a flower and 5% of its power get a Boss Seed (blooms Rare or better); the biggest gets a Champion Seed (Epic or better). Loot needs a free plot. The next boss is sized to the killing blow, so it always takes a real fight. Squeek holders get a 4th plot and +5 power.
